## 6. Perception, identity, and affect

## 7. Memory and dreaming

Raw sensor/evidence records remain distinct from working context and durable memories. Durable items are temporally versioned and support confidence, provenance, correction, supersession, abstention, bounded forgetting, and consolidation. Dreaming may recombine memory into explicitly synthetic content for rehearsal or creativity, but dream output cannot become external fact without later grounded evidence and normal write rules.
