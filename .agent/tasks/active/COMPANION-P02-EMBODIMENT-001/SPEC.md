# SPEC — COMPANION-P02-EMBODIMENT-001

## Primary outcome

A production-capable, identity-locked sprite embodiment candidate running in
Godot 4.7.2 and ready to receive later body-neutral organism intents.

## Mandatory delivered systems

1. Verified approved reference package.
2. Canonical construction, palette, anatomy, and eight-direction model.
3. Deterministic authored-source → MON_FRAME_V1 → atlas/pack pipeline.
4. Versioned MonAnimationClip schema/Rust/Godot representation.
5. At least 32 semantic clip families and 256 unique body-frame sources.
6. Eye/gaze/blink and mouth overlay libraries.
7. Layered MonAvatar and MonAnimationDirector.
8. Embodiment intent/result bridge.
9. Explicit bounded Openbox habitat policy.
10. Automated asset QA and visual review package.
11. 10,000-case transition matrix.
12. Two-hour continuous target-host playback.
13. Deterministic artifact, export/restore, CI, and evidence package.

## Fixed constraints

- Approved purple mon identity and anatomy.
- MON_FRAME_V1.
- Runtime raster sprites; authoring rig may not replace the runtime frame library.
- Godot is nonauthoritative.
- No organism, memory, sensor, speech, model, biometric, notification, or live
  care implementation.
- Bulk generated binary packs are artifacts, not ordinary-Git history.
- Operator visual approval is mandatory and cannot be self-issued.

## Evidence ceiling

Phase 02 may establish bounded engineering and visual candidate evidence only.
It cannot establish aliveness, organism autonomy, perception, caregiving,
security certification, product reliability, or release suitability.
