## Experiment doctrine

- Freeze versions, configs, multiple deterministic and randomized/property seeds, datasets/fixtures, schema/canonicalization/digest versions, persisted start states, boot epochs, kill points, artifact rights, and abort thresholds before running. Retain every reproducing seed.
- Use local ext4/NVMe for live state; SSHFS holds only source and immutable copied results.
- Separate warmup, idle baseline, isolated component, and integrated concurrency.
- Report `PASSED`, `FAILED`, `BLOCKED`, `NOT RUN`, or `NOT APPLICABLE`; retain partial/negative evidence.
- Avoid privileged changes. A missing utility is not missing capability.
- No benchmark may choose a product promise. RQ-04, RQ-08, RQ-09, and RQ-11 retain operator policy decisions.
- The 100 restart/replay cases, 1,000 cycles, and 30-minute run are provisional minimum engineering evidence floors only. They are designed to expose deterministic/recovery, bounded-cycle, and immediate resource/liveness defects respectively; they cannot establish statistical reliability, safety efficacy, product lifetime, endurance, or an SLA.
- Floor coverage must span multiple organism-state boundaries, supported and invalid schema cases, pre/during/post-commit kill points, multiple persisted states, same/cross-boot time, and retained property/randomized seeds. Later gates separately require 24-hour, multi-day, media, shadow-safety, accessibility, pilot, and operational evidence.
- Phase 01 may not start until the Architect approves its exact bounded directive. EXP-00 blocks implementation of the three authoritative services, but not approved language-neutral repository/schema/XDG/logging/health/supervisor/CI foundation work. EXP-01/02/03/04/05 protocols and stop limits must be dispositioned before the dependent implementation begins.
