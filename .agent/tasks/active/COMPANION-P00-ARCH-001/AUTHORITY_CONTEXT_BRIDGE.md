## Project destination that must remain visible

The finished product is one persistent developing digital creature, not a prompt-response chatbot with an avatar. It must have durable identity, needs, drives, temperament, goals, commitments, relationships, memory, learning, sleep/dream consolidation, autonomous life, multimodal social understanding, and causal visual embodiment. It must survive restart and absence, develop through lived teaching, and remain useful under degraded model/network conditions.

A separate caregiving core must conservatively recognize qualified assistance scenarios, verify uncertainty, execute explicit trusted-contact policy, and preserve an auditable incident trail. The companion's mood, personality, or language model may not suppress, fabricate, or independently authorize a safety escalation.

## Foundational adopted constraints

- The project is independent ground zero. Prior failed companion, lifeform, monitoring, or assistant projects are not donors, predecessors, constraints, or default sources of code or design.
- Notion is the single source of truth for project meaning, operator rulings, architecture, risks, directives, and status.
- GitHub is authoritative for versioned repository artifacts, commits, issues, and technical evidence.
- The organism is a hybrid persistent system; an LLM is not the creature, canonical memory, identity, needs, or safety authority.
- Perception outputs uncertain, source-linked, model-versioned evidence. It does not write durable truth or contact people directly.
- Memory is typed, temporal, versioned, correction-aware, source-linked, privacy-classed, and capable of abstention.
- Dream output is synthetic and cannot become factual external memory without real evidence.
- Godot is the embodiment adapter. A renderer failure must not corrupt organism state, memory, consent, credentials, or safety incidents.
- Core identity, organism state, memory, consent, essential interaction, safety policy, backup, export, and restore require a local survival path.
- Code, models, weights, datasets, voices, art, plugins, and services each require separate provenance, rights, security, and replacement analysis.
- Consumer-product security, privacy, recovery, observability, update, and evidence obligations begin at the engineering foundation.

## Operator decisions adopted after the certified ingest

### ADR-33 — First user population and household model

- One adult primary user aged 18 or older who may have support needs.
- Single-primary-user authority model.
- One or more explicitly configured trusted caregivers or contacts.
- Minors are outside iteration one.
- Other household members and visitors are unknown or limited-context unless separately enrolled under later approved rules.

### ADR-34 — Product path and claims

- Develop toward a consumer product from the start.
- Narrow initial claim: a persistent digital companion with configurable check-in and trusted-contact assistance.
- Do not claim diagnosis, guaranteed emergency detection, prevention of harm, continuous medical monitoring, or replacement for emergency services unless separately qualified and legally classified.

### ADR-35 — First caregiving scenario and jurisdiction

- First scenario: explicit spoken help request.
- First jurisdiction: Kentucky, United States.
- Qualification order begins with simulation, recorded replay, and shadow mode before live trusted-contact delivery.
- A declared check-in deadline remains a likely later scenario.
- Fall detection, routine deviation, facial affect, or voice affect are not approved as first standalone triggers.

### ADR-36 — Dedicated display

- Use the separate 1366×768 Openbox-managed output as the iteration-one mon display.
- The two 4K GNOME-managed outputs are not the default mon display.
- Output selection, geometry-change handling, placement persistence, and display-loss recovery must be explicit.

### ADR-37 — Habitat mode

- Use a bounded resizable Godot application window on the dedicated Openbox display.
- Borderless full-screen and transparent desktop overlay are not iteration-one defaults.
- Architecture v1.0 must define initial/minimum/maximum size, aspect and content scaling, mon occupancy, focus/input behavior, position persistence, and recovery/fallback behavior.
