## 7. Workstream B — direct-care IPC producer identity

### 7.1 Threat boundary

The required property is not protection against a fully compromised user account or kernel. It is prevention of unauthorized same-user sibling processes, malformed companion/model/Godot processes, stale producer generations, and accidental clients from creating valid safety candidates under the stated local threat model.

### 7.2 Required candidates

- pathname AF_UNIX socket with private directory/mode, message-declared producer identity, and UID equality only.
- Demonstrate whether an unrelated same-user process can connect and submit a structurally valid candidate. Treat success as expected evidence that this baseline is insufficient.

Candidate 1:

- strict descriptor closure and close-on-exec discipline;
- kernel peer credentials and expected producer generation;
- process non-dumpability and other available nonprivileged confinement;
- restart revokes old channel and creates a new generation.

Candidate 2:

- a distinct host-viable design such as pathname/seqpacket plus supervisor-pinned peer PID or pidfd/generation capability, Linux security labels if already enforceable, or a separately justified cryptographic/capability mechanism.
- Do not choose an unavailable or privilege-only design merely to satisfy the count; if only one design is executable, document the blocked second candidate and the reason.

### 7.3 Required attacks and lifecycle tests

- same-user unauthorized connection and valid-message injection;
- spoofed producer fields;
- malformed and unsupported schema;
- replay and duplicate candidate;
- unintended FD inheritance to sibling/child processes;
- `/proc` or pidfd descriptor duplication paths available without privilege;
- PID reuse or generation mismatch;
- producer crash/restart and old-channel revocation;
- care restart;
- companion stopped and companion database absent/corrupt;
- common sensor/model-producer outage and degraded coverage;
- attempts from companion mood, memory, LLM text, dream, animation, and Godot status message types.

Care must either reject unauthorized input before a safety receipt/incident or the candidate fails. Duplicate valid inputs may produce duplicate-receipt audit evidence but at most one logical policy transition.
