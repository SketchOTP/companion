## Artifact record required before adoption

Name, role, exact version/digest/source, maintainer/release date/support status, code license and notices, model/weight license, dataset/training provenance and license, voice/content rights, asset rights, service terms/DPA/region/retention/training use, transitive inventory, known vulnerabilities, network behavior, target Linux/GPU/CPU fit, update/rollback path, replacement cost, benchmark protocol/result, legal reviewer, and approving ADR.

No table row upgrades a Notion evidence `Candidate` to `Reviewed` or an interim ADR to adopted.
